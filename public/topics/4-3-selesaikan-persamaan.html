📝 Key Formulas & Solution Types

General Form of Homogeneous 2nd Order ODE

a d2ydx2 + b dydx + cy = 0
Auxiliary Equation
am2 + bm + c = 0
Discriminant
Δ = b2 - 4ac
Quadratic Formula
m = -b ± √Δ2a

Three Types of Solutions Based on the Discriminant

Type 1: Real & Distinct
b2 - 4ac > 0
Roots: m₁ ≠ m₂ (both real numbers)
General Solution:
y = Aem₁x + Bem₂x
where A and B are arbitrary constants
Type 2: Real & Equal
b2 - 4ac = 0
Roots: m₁ = m₂ = m
General Solution:
y = (A + Bx)emx
where A and B are arbitrary constants
Type 3: Complex Conjugate
b2 - 4ac < 0
Roots: m = p ± iq
where p = -b2a, q = √|Δ|2a
General Solution:
y = epx(Acos(qx) + Bsin(qx))
where A and B are arbitrary constants

⚠️ Common Mistakes to Avoid
  • Sign errors: Always check signs in the auxiliary equation.
  • Missing x in Type 2: Remember it's (A + Bx)emx, not just (A + B)emx.
  • Complex roots: Separate the real part (p) and imaginary part (q) correctly.
  • Discriminant: It's b² - 4ac, not b² + 4ac.
  • Constants: Always include both A and B in the final answer.
💡 Tips to Remember
  • Memory aid: "DAE" - Discriminant determines Answer's Expression.
  • Verify roots: Substitute them back into the auxiliary equation.
  • Euler's formula: eix = cos(x) + i·sin(x) is the basis for complex root solutions.
  • Count constants: A 2nd order DE will always have 2 arbitrary constants.
  • Practice pattern: Start with easy problems and increase complexity gradually.

🎓 First Order Differential Equation

Integrating Factor Method - Pembelajaran Interaktif

📌 Objektif Pembelajaran:

  • Memahami konsep first order linear differential equation
  • Mengenal pasti standard form persamaan
  • Mengira integrating factor \(\mu(x)\)
  • Menyelesaikan persamaan menggunakan integrating factor method
  • Mengaplikasikan initial condition untuk mencari particular solution

🌍 Kenapa Kita Belajar Ini?

Contoh Dalam Kehidupan Sebenar:

1. Temperature Decay (Penurunan Suhu):

Bayangkan anda meletakkan segelas kopi panas dalam bilik yang sejuk. Suhu kopi akan turun mengikut masa. Perubahan suhu ini boleh diwakili oleh first order differential equation!

Persamaan:

\[ \frac{dT}{dt} + k(T - T_{\text{room}}) = 0 \]

2. Mixing Problems (Masalah Pencampuran):

Dalam industri, kita perlu tahu berapa banyak bahan kimia dalam tangki apabila air masuk dan keluar. First order differential equation membantu kita mengira ini!

3. Circuit Analysis (Analisis Litar Elektrik):

Dalam litar RL atau RC, arus atau voltan berubah mengikut masa dan boleh diselesaikan menggunakan integrating factor method.

📊 Konsep Asas

First Order Differential Equation bermaksud persamaan yang melibatkan:

Kadar Perubahan

\(\dfrac{dy}{dx}\)

Berapa cepat y berubah

→

Fungsi y

\(y\)

Nilai yang kita cari

→

Fungsi Lain

\(f(x)\)

Fungsi yang melibatkan x

📐 Standard Form & Formula

Standard Form:

\[ \frac{dy}{dx} + \textcolor{#f97316}{P(x)}\,y = \textcolor{#10b981}{Q(x)} \]
\(\dfrac{dy}{dx}\)

Derivative pertama y terhadap x (kadar perubahan)

\(P(x)\)

Fungsi dalam sebutan x (coefficient y)

\(y\)

Pembolehubah bersandar (dependent variable)

\(Q(x)\)

Fungsi bebas (independent function)

Integrating Factor \(\mu(x)\):

\[ \textcolor{#f59e0b}{\mu(x)} = e^{\int \textcolor{#f97316}{P(x)}\,dx} \]

Integrating factor adalah "magic multiplier" yang membantu kita selesaikan persamaan!

General Solution:

\[ y \cdot \textcolor{#f59e0b}{\mu(x)} = \int \textcolor{#f59e0b}{\mu(x)} \cdot \textcolor{#10b981}{Q(x)}\,dx + C \]

Atau:

\[ y = \frac{\int \textcolor{#f59e0b}{\mu(x)} \cdot \textcolor{#10b981}{Q(x)}\,dx + C}{\textcolor{#f59e0b}{\mu(x)}} \]

🎯 Langkah-Langkah Penyelesaian

Step 1: Tukar ke Standard Form

\[ \frac{dy}{dx} + \textcolor{#f97316}{P(x)}\,y = \textcolor{#10b981}{Q(x)} \]
↓

Step 2: Cari Integrating Factor

\[ \textcolor{#f59e0b}{\mu(x)} = e^{\int \textcolor{#f97316}{P(x)}\,dx} \]
↓

Step 3: Darab Kedua-dua Belah

\[ \textcolor{#f59e0b}{\mu(x)}\frac{dy}{dx} + \textcolor{#f59e0b}{\mu(x)}\textcolor{#f97316}{P(x)}\,y = \textcolor{#f59e0b}{\mu(x)}\textcolor{#10b981}{Q(x)} \]

Left side becomes \(\dfrac{d}{dx}\!\left(y \cdot \textcolor{#f59e0b}{\mu(x)}\right)\)

↓

Step 4: Integrate Kedua-dua Belah

\[ y \cdot \textcolor{#f59e0b}{\mu(x)} = \int \textcolor{#f59e0b}{\mu(x)} \cdot \textcolor{#10b981}{Q(x)}\,dx + C \]
↓

Step 5: Selesaikan untuk y

\[ y = \frac{\int \textcolor{#f59e0b}{\mu(x)} \cdot \textcolor{#10b981}{Q(x)}\,dx + C}{\textcolor{#f59e0b}{\mu(x)}} \]

  • • Boleh guna kurung atau tidak: e^(-2x) atau e^-2x
  • • Contoh: 2xe^2x-e^2x atau 2xe^(2x)-e^(2x) - semua OK!

📚 Contoh Lengkap

Contoh 1: \(\dfrac{dy}{dx} + \textcolor{#f97316}{3}\,y = \textcolor{#10b981}{6e^{2x}}\), \(y(0) = 2\)

Penyelesaian Step-by-Step

Step 1: Kenal pasti P(x) dan Q(x)
\[ \textcolor{#f97316}{P(x) = 3}, \quad \textcolor{#10b981}{Q(x) = 6e^{2x}} \]
Step 2: Kira integrating factor
\[ \textcolor{#f59e0b}{\mu(x)} = e^{\int \textcolor{#f97316}{3}\,dx} = \textcolor{#f59e0b}{e^{3x}} \]
Step 3: Darab dengan \(\mu(x)\)
\[ \textcolor{#f59e0b}{e^{3x}}\frac{dy}{dx} + 3\textcolor{#f59e0b}{e^{3x}}y = \textcolor{#10b981}{6e^{2x}} \cdot \textcolor{#f59e0b}{e^{3x}} \]
\[ \frac{d}{dx}\!\left(y\,\textcolor{#f59e0b}{e^{3x}}\right) = 6e^{5x} \]
Step 4: Integrate
\[ y\,\textcolor{#f59e0b}{e^{3x}} = \int 6e^{5x}\,dx = \frac{6}{5}e^{5x} + C \]
Step 5: Solve untuk y
\[ y = \frac{\frac{6}{5}e^{5x} + C}{\textcolor{#f59e0b}{e^{3x}}} = \frac{6}{5}e^{2x} + C\textcolor{#f59e0b}{e^{-3x}} \]
Step 6: Apply \(y(0) = 2\)
\[ 2 = \frac{6}{5}e^{0} + Ce^{0} = \frac{6}{5} + C \]
\[ C = 2 - \frac{6}{5} = \frac{4}{5} \]

Penjelasan Visual & Jawapan Akhir

Kenapa \(\textcolor{#f59e0b}{e^{3x}}\)?

Kerana \(P(x) = 3\), dan \(\int 3\,dx = 3x\)

Integration Rule:
\[ \int e^{ax}\,dx = \frac{1}{a}e^{ax} + C \]
\[ \int 6e^{5x}\,dx = \frac{6}{5}e^{5x} \]
Simplifying:

Exponent rule: \(\dfrac{e^{a}}{e^{b}} = e^{a-b}\)

\[ \frac{e^{5x}}{\textcolor{#f59e0b}{e^{3x}}} = e^{2x} \]
Final Answer:
\[ y = \frac{6}{5}e^{2x} + \frac{4}{5}\textcolor{#f59e0b}{e^{-3x}} \]

Contoh 2: \(xy' + \textcolor{#f97316}{2}\,y = \textcolor{#10b981}{4x^{2}}\), \(y(1) = 3\)

Penyelesaian Step-by-Step

Step 1: Tukar ke standard form (Bahagi dgn x)
\[ y' + \textcolor{#f97316}{\frac{2}{x}}\,y = \textcolor{#10b981}{4x} \]
Step 2: Kenal pasti P(x) dan Q(x)
\[ \textcolor{#f97316}{P(x) = \frac{2}{x}}, \quad \textcolor{#10b981}{Q(x) = 4x} \]
Step 3: Kira integrating factor
\[ \textcolor{#f59e0b}{\mu(x)} = e^{\int \frac{2}{x}\,dx} = e^{2\ln|x|} = e^{\ln(x^{2})} = \textcolor{#f59e0b}{x^{2}} \]
Step 4: Darab dan integrate
\[ \frac{d}{dx}\!\left(y \cdot \textcolor{#f59e0b}{x^{2}}\right) = \textcolor{#f59e0b}{x^{2}} \cdot \textcolor{#10b981}{4x} = 4x^{3} \]
\[ y\,\textcolor{#f59e0b}{x^{2}} = \int 4x^{3}\,dx = x^{4} + C \]
Step 5: Solve untuk y
\[ y = \frac{x^{4} + C}{\textcolor{#f59e0b}{x^{2}}} = x^{2} + \frac{C}{\textcolor{#f59e0b}{x^{2}}} \]
Step 6: Apply \(y(1) = 3\)
\[ 3 = 1^{2} + \frac{C}{1^{2}} = 1 + C \implies C = 2 \]

Tips & Tricks & Jawapan Akhir

💡 Tip:

Apabila \(P(x) = \dfrac{a}{x}\), integrating factor akan jadi \(x^{a}\)

Integration \(\mu(x)\):
\[ \int \frac{2}{x}\,dx = 2\ln|x| \]
\[ e^{2\ln|x|} = e^{\ln(x^{2})} = \textcolor{#f59e0b}{x^{2}} \]
Final Answer:
\[ y = x^{2} + \frac{2}{\textcolor{#f59e0b}{x^{2}}} \]

💡 Tips & Common Mistakes

💡 Tips untuk Ingat:

  • Standard Form Dahulu: Sentiasa tukar equation ke form \(\frac{dy}{dx} + P(x)y = Q(x)\) sebelum mulakan. Bahagi dengan coefficient \(y'\) jika perlu!
  • Jangan Lupa "+C": Bila integrate, jangan lupa constant of integration C. Tapi bila kira \(\mu(x)\), kita tak perlu +C kerana ia akan cancel out.
  • Simplify Step by Step: Jangan rush! Simplify setiap step dengan teliti. Banyak kesilapan datang dari algebra yang tergesa-gesa.
  • Check Initial Condition: Pastikan substitute IC dengan betul untuk cari C. Double check calculation!
  • Integration by Parts: Bila Q(x) complicated (seperti \(xe^{x}\)), gunakan integration by parts: \(\int u\,dv = uv - \int v\,du\)

⚠️ Common Mistakes:

  • Lupa Tukar ke Standard Form: Jangan terus cari P(x) tanpa standardkan equation dahulu. Coefficient \(y'\) mestilah 1!
  • Salah Integrate P(x): Bila \(P(x) = \frac{2}{x}\), ramai ter-integrate jadi \(\ln(x)\) instead of \(2\ln(x)\). Jangan lupa coefficient!
  • Simplify \(e^{\ln(x)}\) Salah: Remember: \(e^{\ln(x)} = x\), bukan \(\ln(x)\)!
  • Lupa Darab \(\mu(x)\) pada Q(x): After dapat \(\mu(x)\), mesti darab KEDUA-DUA belah equation, bukan hanya satu side sahaja!
  • Sign Errors: Berhati-hati dengan negative signs, terutama bila simplify fractions atau subtract terms.
  • Lupa Apply Initial Condition: General solution sahaja tak cukup! Mesti apply IC untuk particular solution jika soalan ada IC.
